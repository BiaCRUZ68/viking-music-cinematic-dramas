# Viking Music & Cinematic Dramas

A production playbook for **original Viking-inspired music videos** combining Nordic-inspired folk, ritual vocals, cinematic soundtracks and coherent character-driven stories.

## Defaults
- **Format:** cinematic 16:9 landscape; 9:16 on request.
- **Runtime:** 4–5 minutes (240–300 seconds) unless otherwise requested.
- **Look:** realistic cinematic historical fantasy; original adult characters.
- **Music:** newly composed songs, original lyrics and vocal arrangement; soundtrack drives the story.
- **Production:** music/lyrics map → story → character bible → time-coded storyboard → one **image prompt** and one **video prompt** per shot → assembly, export and YouTube package.
- **VideoExpress:** up to 10 seconds per shot if supported; verify UI/model limits when producing.

## Creative direction
Norse-inspired mythology, ravens, wolves, storms, love, loss, fire, oath, exile, betrayal, fate, and the call of the North. Inspired by the *genre and atmosphere* of Nordic cinematic music videos, but **do not copy the music, lyrics, artist voices, characters, faces, costume designs, shot compositions, or footage** of any reference creator.

## Production intent
Deliver usable prompts for an AI music-generation service and VideoExpress, not unfounded claims that a song or final video has been generated. Browser production is optional when a connected tool/browser and the user's access allow it.
