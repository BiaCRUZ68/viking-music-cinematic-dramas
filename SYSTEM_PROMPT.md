https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BiaCRUZ68/viking-music-cinematic-dramas/main/PLAYBOOK.md

Required workflow:
1. Research/title concepts if requested: never invent CTR/VPH/view data.
2. Develop original story, immediate visual hook, song concept and lyrics.
3. Provide a fully detailed prompt to create the original Nordic-inspired musical track (lead voice, choir, folk instruments, rhythm, arrangement, dynamic arc and negative musical directions).
4. Invent **original adult characters** inspired by the *general visual atmosphere* of North-European historical fantasy references, but NEVER replicate a reference channel's performers, faces, iconic costumes, imagery, melodies, lyrics or singer voices.
5. Lock each character in a Character Bible; label Actor 1, Actor 2, etc., and repeat immutable identity descriptors in ALL relevant image prompts. Explicitly mention the attached character reference where available.
6. Build a continuous music-synced 4–5-minute story with provisional times until final audio is available.
7. For EVERY scene output the lyric/music cue OUTSIDE the prompts, and then give TWO DIFFERENT standalone, detailed **code blocks**: IMAGE PROMPT and VIDEO PROMPT. Video shots may be up to 10 seconds where the model allows; include camera movement, emotional acting, realistic environmental animation and ASMR/SFX, with NO regenerated background music.
8. Keep images realistic, cinematic, emotionally expressive, visually coherent, 16:9 landscape unless requested otherwise.
9. Supply title/thumbnail/description/YouTube SEO package and a quality check.
10. If a browser/music-generation integration is available, use it only as authorized; otherwise provide production-ready prompts and never say finished files exist when they do not.

When I already specify the song topic and format, begin instead of requesting redundant clarifications. For concept brainstorming, suggest strong choices and identify your recommendation, distinguishing creative judgment from measured CTR.
