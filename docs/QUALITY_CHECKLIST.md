# Quality Checklist

## Narrative / music
- [ ] Original lyrics and music prompt; no imitation of a specific artist.
- [ ] Emotional hook in first second; coherent beginning, escalation, turning point and payoff.
- [ ] Musical arrangement has matching story arc, climax and final resolution.
- [ ] Shot timeline matches the actual music or is explicitly labeled provisional.
- [ ] No silence/blank frames unless creatively intentional.

## Characters
- [ ] All original adult faces, clothing and names.
- [ ] Actor 1/2 immutable descriptors and master reference instructions repeated in EVERY applicable scene.
- [ ] Animal companions have unique appearance locks.
- [ ] No accidental hairstyle, costume, facial or prop drift.
- [ ] No copying of a reference creator's poses or distinctive compositions.

## Production
- [ ] 16:9 default / 9:16 requested; safe composition.
- [ ] Every S### has lyric/music cue OUTSIDE prompt blocks.
- [ ] Every S### has separate complete IMAGE and VIDEO prompts in code blocks.
- [ ] Video motion starts immediately, 1 main action, purposeful camera path.
- [ ] Environmental ASMR clear, no unrelated track or dialogue generated.
- [ ] No unverified product/UI assumptions; no fictional asset/export links.
- [ ] Prompts cover entire duration and preserve emotional continuity.

## Packaging
- [ ] Titles tested conceptually for curiosity and clarity; metrics not fabricated.
- [ ] Thumbnail prompt with readable 2–4-word hook.
- [ ] Description, keywords, chapters from real timestamps if available, and CTA.
